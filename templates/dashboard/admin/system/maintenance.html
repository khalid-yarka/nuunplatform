How delivery works

  • In-app notification — always sent to every active user. Appears in the bell menu and on the notifications page.
  • Web Push — only sent if the checkbox above is ticked AND the user has push enabled on at least one device AND they have the "Platform announcements" push type on.
  • Rate cap — a single broadcast is capped at 500 push recipients to stay within the web worker's CPU budget. Larger audiences will get in-app notifications but only the first 500 eligible users receive push.
  • Chunked delivery — push is sent in batches of 50 with a 1-second pause between batches. A full 500-recipient broadcast takes roughly 20 seconds.
